## Selection rule applied

**No identifiable children's faces.** Every image is either a place/landscape or
a hands-only detail shot.

Stock photographs of children on a school homepage imply those children attend
the school. A parent community usually notices, and Unsplash does not guarantee
model releases. Restricting to place and hands avoids the problem entirely while
still meeting the deck's stated themes — hands-on, nature, exploring.

## These are placeholders

The deck notes the image library "needs to be built out further". Before launch
these must be replaced with real, consented photography of Farmhouse children,
educators and the actual North Head campus. In particular:

- `northhead-coast.jpg` is Australian coastline, **but it is not North Head.**
  Using it in production would misrepresent the campus.
- None of these show a Montessori prepared environment or the heritage buildings.
