Growing independence

What your child becomes, year by year.

Our approach is designed to grow with the child. What begins in Preschool isn't the end of the journey — it builds the foundations for the deeper thinking, independence and responsibility that come next.

Age 3 “I can do it myself.”
  • Chooses, carries, pours and puts things back.
  • Stays with something difficult for longer than you might expect.
  • Begins to take responsibility for their own little world.
Age 5 “I can do it myself.”
  • Reads, writes and manages familiar routines with growing confidence.
  • Uses real tools and materials with care.
  • Asks why, and wants to find out.
Age 9 “I can work things out.”
  • Plans work, collaborates and follows through.
  • Explains a point of view — and can change their mind when another idea is stronger.
  • Develops a powerful sense of fairness and belonging.
Age 12 “I can take ownership.”
  • Takes an idea from question to finished project.
  • Knows what they don't know — and how to find out.
  • Forms views, speaks with confidence, and can explain the thinking behind them.

Preschool builds the foundations. Primary opens up a much bigger world.

Concentration, choice, order and independence come first. Then those foundations meet deeper exploration, reasoning and creativity.

Life at North Head

A sanctuary as part of an ordinary school day.

Our Primary campus sits within North Head Sanctuary, so bushland, headland and ocean air are part of the everyday. Exploring is something children do, not something they read about.

We work alongside the Harbour Trust as custodian of North Head, caring for its heritage buildings and bringing children, learning and community life to the place we call home.
