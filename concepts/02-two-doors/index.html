One Farmhouse

Two campuses. One continuous journey.

Farmhouse brings together Preschool and Primary through a shared philosophy, a strong sense of community, and a belief in nurturing each child's curiosity, independence and confidence.

Each campus is shaped around the developmental needs of the children within it — creating two experiences with their own character, while remaining part of one continuous Farmhouse journey.

We are a small, not-for-profit community school. Every child is known individually, and treated that way.

Growing independence, from three to twelve.

Age 3“I can do it myself.”

Chooses, carries, pours and puts things back. Stays with something difficult longer than you might expect.

Age 5“I can do it myself.”

Reads, writes and manages routines with growing confidence. Uses real tools with care. Asks why.

Age 9“I can work things out.”

Plans work, collaborates and follows through. Can change their mind when another idea is stronger.

Age 12“I can take ownership.”

Takes an idea from question to finished project. Knows what they don't know — and how to find out.

Getting started

Three steps, and we'll walk you through each one.

1. Book a tour

The best way to understand Farmhouse is to see a classroom in action. Tours run regularly at both campuses, and private tours are available on request.

Days & times — to be confirmed

2. Visit and apply

After your tour you'll be invited to apply. Parents are invited to observe, and we'll arrange a visit for your child suited to the classroom they'd be joining.

3. Settling in

Once a place is offered we'll walk you and your child through settling in, so the first day feels like coming back rather than starting fresh.
