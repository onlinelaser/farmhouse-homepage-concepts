On the two playful concepts

  • They tilt toward the Preschool end of the brand. Primary is meant to read grounded and confident, Preschool warm and playful. A fun whole-school front door leans on the softer half of the system — worth weighing against families who are looking for visible proof of academic rigour.
  • Concept E's timetable is invented structure. The page carries a visible reviewer banner saying exactly that. The shape of the day must be confirmed with the school before anyone outside sees it.
  • Concept E fills a gap the others don't. Plenty of families don't understand what a school day actually looks like, and aren't ready to commit to a full tour to find out. No other concept answers that directly.

What to know before you review

  • All five demote Montessori. The current live site leads with “Discover the difference of Montessori” and carries a six-page Montessori nav section. These concepts reverse that — outcomes first, method second — so “Montessori” is dissolved into “Our Approach” throughout.
  • No invented testimonials. There are no consented parent quotes yet, so none appear. Fake quotes in a stakeholder review have a habit of surviving into production.
  • Photography is placeholder Unsplash imagery, marked as such on every image. Chosen against the deck's stated themes — hands-on, nature, exploring — with brand-colour scrims over each photo so the campus colour expressions still read.
  • No identifiable children's faces appear anywhere. Every image is a place, a landscape, or a hands-only detail. Stock photos of children imply those children attend the school, a parent community usually notices, and Unsplash does not guarantee model releases. Restricting to place and hands avoids that entirely.
  • The coastal image is Australian coastline, but it is not North Head. Fine for a review mockup, misleading in production. Real photography of the actual campus has to replace it. See assets/images/CREDITS.md.
  • Tour times are marked TBC — the deck doesn't state them.
  • Value proposition text is never pasted onto the page. The deck is explicit that value props are internal and “rarely appear word for word in public”. Public copy is derived from them.

Open items for the brand team

  • No master Farmhouse lockup exists. Drive holds only Primary and Preschool variants. These mockups use the icon from the Primary lockup as the whole-school mark — defensible because its seven petals carry all eight brand colours, four Primary and four Preschool.
  • The Preschool logo is off-palette. It uses #faa933 #4b9b50 #52b4cb #5a74a3 #bc8dbf #c36c3a #b9c246 #b88d34 — none of which appear in the 2026 deck. Likely a legacy asset.
  • Grey resolved. The deck lists Grey as #ede7d7, identical to Eggshell — a typo. The logos use #616d78, adopted here.
  • Gold and the Preschool pastels fail WCAG AA as text (2.02:1 and below on Eggshell). They are used as accents, fills and backgrounds only, never for type.
